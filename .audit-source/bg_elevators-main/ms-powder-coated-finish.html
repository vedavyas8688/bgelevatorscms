MS Powder coated finish

Product Overview

MS Powder coated finish is a design created to reflect your Building Richness.

This marvelleous model comes with a complete Mirror Finish Car Enclosure with a False Ceiling fitted with Blower for a cooling effect inside the Car. The Mirror Finish Cabin comes with Fully Automatic Doors available in both Centre Opening or Telescopic doors.

Beyond aesthetics, this cabin is designed with a strong emphasis on safety and functionality. It includes plasma indicators inside the car, LED digital indicators at each floor, and SS90 square buttons for reliable and easy operation. All faceplates are finished in stainless steel hairline for a consistent premium look. To enhance passenger protection, the cabin comes with electronic door safety sensors as a standard feature. Optional upgrades with unique stainless steel textures are also available to suit custom interiors.
