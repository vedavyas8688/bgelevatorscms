What Should Builders Ask Lift Manufacturers in Bangalore Before Starting a Project?

A structural drawing lands on your desk with a rectangle marked “LIFT” and nothing else inside it. No pit depth, no headroom, no hint of whether that shaft suits a machine-room-less unit or a hydraulic one. That rectangle decides more of your project cost than most builders expect, which is why the first serious conversation with BG Elevators and other lift manufacturers in Bangalore belongs before the raft is poured. BG Elevators helps builders understand the key specifications and planning considerations before elevator installation begins.




Start With the Shaft, Not the Brochure

Most early meetings go wrong the same way. The builder asks for a price, the supplier asks how many floors, and both skip the civil detail that governs everything downstream. Begin instead with the hole in the building.

Ask what pit depth the model needs, what overhead it demands above the top landing slab, and what clear internal shaft dimensions the quotation assumes. A six-passenger lift commonly wants a clear shaft around 1.7 m square, a pit near 1.4 m and overhead close to 4.2 m, but each figure moves with drive type and speed. Get them marked against your actual drawing.

Then ask the uncomfortable question: what if the site pours the pit 100 mm shallow, as sites frequently do? Some suppliers absorb it with a reduced-pit design. Others hand you a chipping bill and a three-week delay. Good lift manufacturers in Bangalore tell you which of the two they are before you sign.

Capacity, Traffic and Why Lift Manufacturers in Bangalore Ask About Occupancy

Capacity gets quoted in persons, but it is engineered in kilograms. Six persons is 408 kg, eight is 544 kg, thirteen is 884 kg. If a supplier only talks in person, push for the kilogram rating and the rated speed. Those two numbers decide the motor, the ropes and the shaft loads your structural consultant needs.

Sizing a Residential Tower Honestly

One eight-passenger lift at 1 m/s suits a G+4 block with two flats per floor. Put the same lift in a G+12 tower with four flats per floor and residents queue every morning between 8 and 9.30. Ask how handling capacity over a five-minute peak was calculated for your building. If the answer stays vague, that is information too.

Lift Manufacturer Questions That Reveal Real Experience

A few lift manufacturer questions separate people who have commissioned lifts here from people reading a price list:

  • Which installations nearby can I visit, and how old are they?
  • What is your average breakdown response time in this pin code?
  • Do you stock control boards and door operators locally?
  • Who erects the equipment, your own crew or a subcontractor?

That last one matters more than builders assume. Erection quality drives ride comfort, door alignment and how often someone gets trapped.

Ask lift manufacturers in Bangalore to quote two of these against the same shaft so you can see what you gain and give up. A hydraulic unit might save 300 mm of overhead on a height-restricted plot, then cost more every month for twenty years.

Power Supply, ARD and Local Realities

Ask for the connected load in kW, the starting current, and whether the drive is regenerative. Then ask what happens during an outage. City supply is steadier than it once was, but short interruptions still occur, particularly during monsoon squalls in June and again in October.

An Automatic Rescue Device moves the car to the nearest landing and opens the doors on power failure. Confirm whether it is included or an add-on, how long the battery holds charge, and whether it handles a fully loaded car. Check the DG changeover time too, because a lift that trips on every switchover generates more complaints than anything else in the building.

Compliance, Licensing and Inspection

Lifts in Karnataka fall under state lift legislation, and installations are expected to conform to IS 14665 for design, safety and testing. Ask who prepares the licence application, who attends the inspection, and whether that cost sits inside the quotation.

Get clarity on documentation too: general arrangement drawing, load calculations, test certificates for ropes and safety gear, operating manual. Owners chasing these two years later, usually because an insurer asked, is an avoidable annoyance. Established lift manufacturers in Bangalore hand over a complete file at commissioning, and the better lift manufacturers in Bangalore keep a copy on record.

Elevator Project Planning: Lead Times and Site Readiness

Ask for manufacturing lead time and erection duration separately. Sound elevator project planning allows several weeks for production after drawing approval, then a further stretch on site. Delays rarely come from the factory. They come from a shaft that isn’t plumb, scaffolding that hasn’t been struck, or a three-phase supply that arrives a month late.

Freezing Lift Specifications Early

Lift specifications should be locked before the shaft walls rise: cabin finish, door type and width, floor-to-floor heights, landing door fire rating, control panel position. Late changes to door width are the most expensive revision in elevator installation planning, because the opening is already cast.

Coordinating on a Live Site

Agree in writing who provides scaffolding, hoisting, temporary power, shaft lighting and equipment security. These small items cause most of the friction with lift manufacturer services teams, and they belong in the elevator installation planning schedule.

What Lift Manufacturers in Bangalore Should Include in the Price

Compare quotations line by line, not on the bottom figure. Confirm what sits inside the number:

  1. Equipment, erection, testing and commissioning
  2. Cabin finish and ceiling specification, not just “SS hairline”
  3. ARD, overload sensor, door safety curtain, intercom, fireman’s switch
  4. Licence fees, inspection charges, first-year AMC scope
  5. Warranty period and what voids it

An indicative range for a standard passenger lift in a mid-rise apartment building starts at a few lakh rupees for a basic machine-room-less unit and climbs with speed, capacity or cabin finish. Treat every figure as specification-dependent until a site survey confirms it, and ask which lift manufacturer services continue after warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How early should builders approach lift manufacturers in Bangalore?

Ideally at structural design stage, before foundation work starts. Pit depth, overhead and shaft dimensions feed straight into your drawings. Involving a supplier after the shaft is cast means either changing the model to suit the hole or breaking concrete, and both cost money nobody budgeted for.

2. What shaft size does a six-passenger lift need?

Roughly 1.7 m by 1.7 m clear internal, with a pit near 1.4 m and overhead around 4.2 m, though these shift with drive type, speed and door width. Confirm exact figures against the specific model quoted rather than a general table.

3. Is a machine-room-less lift always the cheaper option?

Not always in first cost, but usually across its life. It saves the machine room slab and the built-up area, and gearless drives draw less power than older geared or hydraulic systems. Hydraulic still wins where overhead height is severely restricted or the building has only two stops.

4. Who is responsible for the lift licence and inspection?

That depends entirely on your contract, which is exactly why it must be written down. Many suppliers prepare the application and attend inspection within their scope; others treat it as an extra. Confirm responsibility, fee and expected timeline before you release the advance payment.

Conclusion

The questions worth asking lift manufacturers in Bangalore are rarely about brand or brochure. They are about pit depth, kilograms, connected load, who erects the equipment, who applies for the licence, and what happens the first time a resident is stuck between floors on a Sunday evening. Get those answered in writing at design stage and the lift stops being a risk item.

Keep the specification sheet filed with your drawings, share it with your structural and electrical consultants, and revisit it before the shaft is cast.
