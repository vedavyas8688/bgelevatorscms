Our Range of Solutions

Smart Vertical Solutions for Every Need

BG Elevators offers smart elevator solutions for residential, commercial, and heavy-duty spaces — combining safety, efficiency, and custom design

Passenger Elevator

Designed for comfort, speed, and style, our Passenger Elevators cater to residential and commercial buildings alike. Whether it’s a high-rise structure or a mid-level facility, our solutions ensure smooth, silent, and safe vertical transport with customizable features to match your building's aesthetic and functional requirements.

Hospital Elevator

Specially engineered for medical environments, our Hospital Elevators prioritize space, hygiene, and reliability. Built to accommodate stretchers, wheelchairs, and medical equipment, these elevators offer precise leveling, smooth movement, and are designed to support critical hospital workflows with maximum safety and ease of use.

Goods Elevator

Our Goods Elevators are engineered to meet the rigorous demands of industrial and commercial spaces. Designed for transporting heavy materials, they feature high load capacity, sturdy construction, and smooth vertical movement. Whether for warehouses, factories, or retail storage areas, these elevators offer reliable performance, durability, and low maintenance.

MRL Elevator

MRL (Machine Room Less) has an advantage of saving power upto 30%. Its absolutely noiseless, smoothest and quicker ride ever for which the passenger enjoys the ride.

Though its little more in price when compared to MR, customer feels its worth for its flawless movement. It does not need a machine room.

Home Elevator

Our Home Elevators are crafted to elevate everyday living with comfort, elegance, and convenience. Ideal for multi-level homes and villas, these lifts are compact, stylish, and operate quietly. We offer fully customized designs that seamlessly match your interior aesthetics while prioritizing safety features like smooth stops, backup power, and child-friendly operation.

Hydraulic Elevator

Hydraulic elevators are mostly used in the houses with 4-5 floors. Customer prefers this type when they have limitations in the space on the top and bottom as it consumes less space,customised for client requirements.

It operates with little lower speed than the traction elevators.
