10 Easy Elevator Maintenance Tasks to Keep It Working Well??

In today’s urban infrastructure, elevators are not just vertical transportation systems—they’re lifelines in homes, apartments, commercial buildings, and hospitals. Whether it's a residential duplex or a 20-floor office tower, keeping elevators in excellent working condition is crucial for safety, convenience, and long-term value.

Yet many property owners overlook the importance of basic, routine maintenance. While major repairs and technical diagnostics should always be handled by professionals like the Best Elevator Company in Bangalore, there are many simple upkeep tasks that can significantly extend the life of your elevator and prevent costly downtime.

Reliable providers such as BG Elevators, recognized as both the Best Lift Company in Bangalore and a leader in installation and service, emphasize that proactive care is the key to elevator longevity. In this blog, we’ll guide you through 10 easy elevator maintenance tasks that anyone responsible for a building or home elevator can follow to keep it running smoothly between service visits.

Maintain a Clean Elevator Cabin for Optimal Performance

Regularly cleaning the elevator cabin isn’t just about appearances—it plays a vital role in elevator maintenance. Dirt, dust, and moisture can harm flooring, buttons, sensors, and lighting, leading to performance issues over time.

Key Areas to Clean:

  • Walls and handrails – Wipe down daily or weekly to remove smudges and bacteria
  • Flooring – Vacuum or mop depending on the surface type
  • Control panel – Gently clean with a soft, dry cloth to avoid damage
  • Mirrors and glass panels – Use non-abrasive cleaners for a streak-free finish 

Avoid using harsh chemicals or spraying liquids directly onto panels or openings. A clean elevator enhances the rider experience and helps protect delicate electronic components—a practice recommended by the Best Elevator Company in Bangalore

1. Inspect and Clean Door Tracks

One of the most common causes of elevator malfunction is debris in the door tracks. Dust, pebbles, wrappers, or even coins can block door sensors and prevent doors from opening or closing properly.

DIY Task:

  • Use a brush or vacuum to clean tracks 
  • Inspect for rust or misalignment
  • Ensure smooth door glide without resistance

Regular door care minimizes emergency calls and is a critical task promoted by the Best Lift Company in Bangalore for all multi-user buildings

2. Best Lift Company in Bangalore – Ensure Safe & Stylish Elevator Lighting

Dim or flickering lights not only affect the look of your elevator—they also signal electrical wear or ballast issues. In some cases, malfunctioning lights can disrupt infrared sensors or safety circuits.

To Do:

  • Inspect cabin lights monthly
  • Replace flickering or burned-out bulbs
  • Opt for energy-efficient LED lighting

Providers like BG Elevators encourage LED upgrades, which reduce electricity usage and maintenance frequency—a hallmark of top-tier elevator services in Bangalore.

3. Listen for Unusual Noises

Elevators should operate quietly. Squeaks, rattles, grinding, or humming noises could indicate issues with the motor, pulley system, or guide rails.

How to Monitor:

  • Ride the elevator weekly during idle times
  • Note changes in sound during operation
  • Report new or persistent noises promptly

Elevator professionals such as the Best Elevator Company in Bangalore often use these audio cues during preventive maintenance checks. Early detection saves time and money.

4. Test the Emergency Alarm and Intercom

Every elevator is equipped with an emergency system—usually a bell, alarm button, or intercom. These systems can be life-saving, but they must function properly.

Monthly Testing Checklist:

  • Press the emergency button to check alarm function
  • Test intercom communication with designated phone number
  • Backup power systems for operation during outages

Building managers should follow manufacturer instructions or coordinate with service teams from the Best Lift Company in Bangalore for advanced testing.

Observe Door Operation Timing

Doors that close too fast or fail to stay open long enough for passengers can cause accidents. Monitoring door operation ensures passenger comfort and accessibility.

Observe For:

  • Door open and close speed 
  • Smoothness and sound of door movement 
  • Re-opening if obstructed (sensor test)

BG Elevators, recognized for installing the best elevators in Bangalore, calibrates doors for safe, ADA-compliant timing. Observing the doors regularly helps you catch early issues.

6. Keep the Machine Room Clean and Accessible

For buildings with a dedicated elevator machine room, this space must be clean, dry, and accessible. Obstructions, temperature extremes, or moisture can damage core components.

Tips:

  • Keep room free from storage items 
  • Ensure ventilation is working
  • Check for signs of leakage, rodents, or dust accumulation

This area should be inspected monthly, and deep-cleaned quarterly with the help of experts like the Best Elevator Company in Bangalore.

7. Ensure Elevator Signage is Visible and Up-to-Date

Proper signage inside and outside the elevator is required by law and improves safety for all users.

Signage Checklist:

  • Load capacity label 
  • Emergency contact information
  • Manufacturer service label with inspection date
  • Fire safety instructions (where applicable)

The Best Lift Company in Bangalore always installs code-compliant signage, but ongoing visual inspections should ensure they remain legible and secured

8. Review Operation Logs and Usage Patterns

If you’re managing a commercial or apartment building, tracking elevator usage can help you understand wear patterns and plan maintenance more effectively.

How to Track:

  • Maintain a logbook for service dates, breakdowns, and alarms
  • Note peak hours and traffic patterns 
  • rack recurring issues for reporting

Many smart systems now offered by providers of the best elevators in Bangalore include digital dashboards for easier monitoring

9. OSchedule Preventive Maintenance Consistently

The most important maintenance task isn’t DIY—it’s ensuring professional servicing is scheduled and consistent. Preventive maintenance checks by licensed engineers catch problems before they escalate.

Professional Tasks Include:

  • Control panel diagnostics
  • Cable tension checks
  • Safety brake and governor testing
  • Lubrication of rails and gears
  • Battery backup inspection

Choose a certified provider like BG Elevators, who are known for high-quality, timely service plans and top-rated elevator services in Bangalore. Never wait for a breakdown to call your service provider.
